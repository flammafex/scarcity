# Scarcity project-owner decisions

**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** Normative project-owner decision record

## Established decisions

1. Scarcity is sovereign private citizen cash. It should compete with Cashu
   and Fedimint as an alternative within Sophia’s distributed OS.
2. Freebird is mandatory anonymous citizenship and eligibility for Scarcity
   participation. It is economy-blind and is not the money.
3. Witness is the time and finality authority. HyperToken provides local-first
   state. Universal relays are transport and have no authority.
4. V2 is a semantic update derived from V1 and the ecosystem, not a total
   rewrite. Legacy V2 documents and plans are non-authoritative.
5. Demurrage is core monetary law. The first profile is a native civic
   currency with permanent burn as its demurrage destination. Rate and epoch
   mechanics require simulation.
6. Bridges and HTLCs are deferred until the demurrage-aware cash kernel is
   correct.
7. The future authority order is: approved owner decisions, then derived
   specifications and vectors, then implementation. Proposals and checklists
   are not authority.

## Open implementation parameters

The established decisions do not yet fix the following implementation
parameters:

- demurrage rate, epoch mechanics, and rounding;
- issuance and liquidity mechanics;
- governance mechanics;
- privacy, metadata, and deployment profile; and
- first federation deployment.
