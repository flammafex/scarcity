# Scarcity synthesis matrix (proposal)

This is an evidence-and-proposal document for owner approval. It is **not
normative**: neither legacy V2 documents nor any `.slim` file is treated as
authority. “Sibling ecosystem evidence” below cites current sibling project
sources directly. A sibling role not implemented by those sources is labelled
as a proposal, not a fact. All V2 language below is proposed unless explicitly
marked as a V1 fact.

| Topic | V1 fact (with concrete repo paths) | Sophia ecosystem evidence (current sibling sources; not authority) | Proposed V2 synthesis | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product / sovereignty thesis; Cashu and Fedimint positioning | Scarcity is Chaumian, private, bearer e-cash without a blockchain, mining, or global ledger; it owns token IDs, amounts, secrets, ownership proofs, arithmetic, and demurrage: [`README.md`](../README.md#L1-L5). | The repository exposes separate Freebird, Witness, and HyperToken adapters in [`src/index.ts`](../src/index.ts#L17-L19); it contains no Cashu or Fedimint implementation. | **Proposal:** position Scarcity as a sovereign cash kernel and interoperability layer, informed by (but not a fork of) Cashu and Fedimint; keep economic rules inside the kernel rather than delegating them to admission or transport. | Open decision |
| Bearer ownership and note privacy | Possession is ownership; there are no addresses, per-token secrets, recipient commitments, and ownership proofs: [`README.md`](../README.md#L109-L119), [`src/ownership.ts`](../src/ownership.ts#L1-L5), [`src/types.ts`](../src/types.ts#L54-L64). | Freebird’s current project description says its client finalizes bearer tokens and its verifier records nullifiers for single use: [`../../freebird/README.md`](../../freebird/README.md#L1-L12). | **Proposal:** retain private bearer notes and local ownership material, but make this model an owner decision rather than a settled V2 assumption; bind proofs only to what settlement needs. | Open decision |
| Citizen / admission role | V1 mint constructs a token from amount, secret, and the supplied admission client at [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L377-L403). Spend-side admission is separately verified as a required, potentially single-use credential at [`src/validator.ts`](../src/validator.ts#L165-L181). | Freebird documents blinded issuance, bearer-token finalization, verifier checking, and nullifier recording: [`../../freebird/README.md`](../../freebird/README.md#L1-L15). It is an admission/token service, not Scarcity economic state. | **Proposal:** decide whether Freebird admission is mandatory or optional for mint and spend; if used, keep it economy-blind. “Citizen” is a proposed admission role, not an owner identity, monetary issuer, or governance authority. | Open decision |
| Monetary unit, issuer, supply, redemption / obligation circuit | V1 `ScarbuckToken.mint()` accepts an unrestricted numeric amount and creates a token: [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L386-L403). Integer and positive-value parsing occurs only at the CLI boundary: [`src/cli/commands/token.ts`](../src/cli/commands/token.ts#L96-L108). README calls supply and amounts Scarcity state: [`README.md`](../README.md#L19-L26). | Freebird’s documented issuer/verifier issue and verify bearer tokens, not a Scarcity monetary unit: [`../../freebird/README.md`](../../freebird/README.md#L1-L12). No monetary authority or redemption service is implemented here. | **Proposal:** define one owner-approved unit and an explicit issuance, liquidity, redemption, and obligation circuit; keep Freebird out of denomination and supply accounting. Do not infer a named monetary authority. | Open decision |
| Witness time, atomic exclusive-spend finality, certificates | Transfer hashes are timestamped by Witness and nullifiers are published to gossip; validation checks Witness deterministically after gossip: [`README.md`](../README.md#L80-L87), [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L150-L159). | Witness currently describes a quorum timestamper: clients submit only hashes and receive threshold-signed attestations, with verification and optional anchoring—not an exclusive-spend finality service: [`../../witness/README.md`](../../witness/README.md#L1-L16), [`../../witness/README.md`](../../witness/README.md#L95-L105). | **Proposal:** decide whether Witness certificates provide atomic exclusive-spend finality or timestamp-only evidence. If finality is selected, define certificate validity, quorum, ordering, conflict, and replay rules in the cash-kernel contract. | Open decision |
| HyperToken local-first wallet state / universal relay boundary | Wallet keys and wallet metadata are persisted locally in `~/.scarcity/wallets.json`: [`src/cli/wallet.ts`](../src/cli/wallet.ts#L13-L33), [`src/cli/wallet.ts`](../src/cli/wallet.ts#L49-L75). Gossip carries nullifiers and proofs: [`src/types.ts`](../src/types.ts#L161-L167). | HyperToken documents an Automerge CRDT, deterministic replay, offline-first convergence, and peer sync without a server: [`../../hypertoken/README.md`](../../hypertoken/README.md#L1-L21). Its example also shows a relay connection, but does not make a relay a consensus authority: [`../../hypertoken/README.md`](../../hypertoken/README.md#L90-L101). | **Proposal:** decide whether HyperToken owns wallet-state replication, and whether a universal relay boundary is required. Candidate design: local-first private notes, encrypted/replay-safe transport, and no relay custody or ledger authority. | Open decision |
| Demurrage | V1 uses lazy expiry: tokens expire if not transferred, with a roughly 576-day default and shared age checks: [`src/constants.ts`](../src/constants.ts#L7-L23), [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L67-L78). | Witness provides timestamps; no sibling demurrage destination, rate, or epoch rule is evidenced here. | **Candidate proposal (not settled):** use Witness-time, permanent burn-only demurrage; transfer does not reset the age clock. The destination of any value reduction, rate, epochs, and whether the owner instead selects a non-burn allocation remain open; a non-burn allocation must not be called “burn.” | Open decision |
| Monetary governance; possible Prestige relevance | V1 code has no monetary governance module; admission and witness are adapter contracts: [`src/types.ts`](../src/types.ts#L181-L208). | Prestige directly documents anonymous, verifiable voting, secret ballots, eligibility gates, nullifiers, and publicly verifiable tallies: [`../../prestige/README.md`](../../prestige/README.md#L1-L7), [`../../prestige/README.md`](../../prestige/README.md#L136-L140). Clout and Rendezvous document social membership and private matching respectively, not monetary governance: [`../../clout/README.md`](../../clout/README.md#L1-L3), [`../../rendezvous/README.md`](../../rendezvous/README.md#L1-L8). | **Proposal:** consider Prestige’s anonymous voting as a future governance mechanism or input, never as an implicit monetary authority or identity link; define activation, capture resistance, and privacy boundaries before enabling it. | Open decision |
| Privacy, collusion, and metadata claim | V1 claims anonymous admission, unlinkable transfers, no addresses, Tor option, and encrypted peer communication, while acknowledging gossip/Witness validation: [`README.md`](../README.md#L109-L119). | HyperToken E2E encryption protects messages from relay servers, but the repository does not prove resistance to colluding issuers, witnesses, relays, or timing observers: [`src/integrations/hypertoken.ts`](../src/integrations/hypertoken.ts#L1-L10). | **Proposal:** state a bounded claim: economic unlinkability and bearer privacy under the selected non-collusion assumptions; separately enumerate metadata leakage and collusion between Freebird, Witness, HyperToken, and any future ecosystem service. | Open decision |
| Bridges and HTLCs | Cross-federation bridge is implemented as source lock plus target mint: [`src/bridge.ts`](../src/bridge.ts#L116-L125). HTLC nullifier publication is deliberately deferred until claim/refund: [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L799-L865). | Witness and gossip are used on both bridge sides; no separate Sophia bridge authority is implemented: [`src/bridge.ts`](../src/bridge.ts#L21-L40). | **Proposal:** decide whether bridges and HTLCs are deferred from launch; if retained, revalidate their lock, claim/refund, certificate, and demurrage semantics against the V2 cash kernel. | Open decision |
| Reusable implementation primitives | Existing primitives include token split/merge/multi-party transfer, ownership proofs, nullifiers, Witness attestations, gossip validation, Tor, bridges, and HTLCs: [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L107-L147), [`src/index.ts`](../src/index.ts#L1-L55). | Adapter contracts isolate external services (`AdmissionClient`, `WitnessClient`, `GossipNetwork`): [`src/types.ts`](../src/types.ts#L181-L208); canonical Witness normalization is in [`src/integrations/witness.ts`](../src/integrations/witness.ts#L110-L127). | **Proposal:** decide which V1 primitives are reused, reworked, or discarded; candidate reuse is limited to interfaces and tested serialization/verification boundaries, with V2 semantics revalidated rather than inherited from legacy documents. | Open decision |
| Frozen V2 assumptions | V1’s concrete assumptions are local token economics, bearer notes, source creation timestamps, and nullifier-based spending: [`src/types.ts`](../src/types.ts#L54-L64), [`src/token.ts`](../src/token.ts#L84-L99). | The current sibling roles above are evidence, not a V2 authority; this repository’s Witness marker is `sophia/v1`: [`src/types.ts`](../src/types.ts#L32-L42). | **Proposal:** freeze only after approval: private bearer notes; the selected Freebird admission mode; the selected Witness timestamp/finality mode; the selected HyperToken wallet and relay roles; the selected demurrage profile; and no bridges/HTLCs in the cash-kernel launch. | Open decision |

## Decisions requested from the project owner

1. Unit and redemption/obligation circuit.
2. Demurrage destination, rate, and epochs, including whether permanent burn-only is the default candidate or another allocation is chosen.
3. Issuance and liquidity rule.
4. Mandatory versus optional Freebird admission for mint and spend.
5. Witness exclusive-spend finality versus timestamp-only evidence.
6. HyperToken’s wallet-state role.
7. Whether a universal relay boundary is required.
8. Bearer-note and ownership model.
9. Whether and when monetary governance activates (including any Prestige role).
10. Exact privacy and collusion profile, including accepted metadata leakage.
11. First polity/deployment.
12. Whether bridges and HTLCs are deferred from launch.
13. Which V1 primitives are reused, reworked, or discarded.
14. Extension priority after the cash kernel (if any capability remains after that disposition).

## Authority statement

User-approved decisions are the only future authority. This matrix is evidence
plus proposals, and does not itself approve, freeze, or normatively define V2.
